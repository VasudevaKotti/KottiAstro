## Order of analysis

- Score the planets first (Light, Performance, Resource, giving Capacity), then the houses (occupant, lord, karaka), then read the running dasha by timing. `[0:07:12]`
- Check retrogressions (vakratva) first. They can also be followed dynamically, but the scoring here is done on the natal chart. `[0:08:15]`
- A planet's own scores depend only on the planet and its own star lord. How many other planets sit in that star lord's nakshatras doesn't matter there; that question belongs to the house and dasha flow. `[1:04:12]`
- At the dasha step, say whether the dasha gives yoga straight or inverted and which houses it acts on (e.g. a negative 7th in the Sun dasha: marriage late or difficult). `[0:58:08]`

## House scoring: occupant, lord, karaka

- A house scores 100: occupant 60, lord 20, karaka 20. Each column is the planet's final score (Capacity) times its weight; a score of 22 gives 13.2 as occupant and 4.4 as lord. The house total is the plain sum. `[0:25:29]`
- Occupied house: the occupant fills the occupant column. With several occupants, take the most powerful planet in the sign (see the leading-planet rules below). `[0:26:29]`
- Empty house, Rule 4 (R4): if exactly one planet sits in the house lord's nakshatras, it acts as the occupant. For Gemini lagna, the Moon alone in Punarvasu would run an empty 7th. `[1:16:23]`
- If that single planet is debilitated, ignore it, as in the dasha flow. `[1:17:24]`
- If two or more planets sit in the lord's nakshatras, or none, the lord itself (following its own star lord) fills the occupant column. An empty house then has the same planet as occupant and lord. `[0:26:29]`
- Enter each planet's final score directly, in every column. Don't add its star lord again (the star lord is already inside the score), and never chain on to the star lord's own star lord. `[0:27:30]`
- Manual floor: the lord and karaka columns get at least 10 of 20 even when the planet fails, and about 15 when it links to the 11th (Mars via Rahu). Calculator shows the plain product (22 × 0.2 = 4.4; 43.2 × 0.2 = 8.64, captions "86.5" `(?)`). `[0:28:32]`
- Own star, own sign or parivartana carries a 60% minimum guarantee, so an occupant in its own star gets at least 36 of 60. For Saturn in its own star 40 was given, against 26.4 by plain calculation (captions also "32, 27" `(?)`); the own star protects the house (here the 6th, from disease). Calculator doesn't apply this yet; the exception still has to be written into the rules. `[1:21:27]`
- Karakas in the table are generic and relationship-based (lagna: Sun). For a specific matter use that matter's karaka, e.g. Mars for land in the 4th rather than the mother's karaka. `[1:14:21]`
- Reading a house: the 7th at about 42% (13.2 + 13 + 16) with inverted planets means trouble in marriage but good public dealings. The dasha timing decides whether it happens. `[1:12:19]`

## Inverted results

- A lagna-malefic (lagna papi, not a yogakaraka) with a retrograde fault gives inverted results: it spoils its bhava (relationships) and gives yoga (money, position). Mark it with a star. `[0:14:22]`
- A yogakaraka of the lagna with the same fault is not inverted; both sides are lost and it gets no star. `[0:23:28]`
- Inverted score = 100 + (100 − straight score). By default the home side and the outside side are both 100; inversion pushes one down and the other up by the same amount, so what is denied at home is found outside. Captions: "22 → 88 lost → 188"; 100 − 22 is 78, so 178, and the calculator appears to show 177.99 `(?)`. `[0:22:26]`
- The same formula applies per house column: inverted value = 2 × column maximum − value (occupant 13.2 of 60 → 106.8, shown as 106). `[1:07:14]`
- Inversion follows the planet. When a planet is starred in the planet table, star it in every house column where it appears (the Sun as 3rd lord); no star, no inversion. `[1:08:14]`
- Invert only the columns whose planet is inverted, then add. Calculator inverts all three columns together (161 for the 3rd house here); the corrected total given was 106 + 25 = 131 `(?)` (which column drops out is unclear). `[0:33:40]`
- A lagna-malefic stays inverted even when a Light exception restores its Light (Mars in Rahu's star with Rahu in the 11th: Light 80, still inverted). `[0:30:33]`
- Rahu or Ketu as the lagna's yogakaraka (Ketu for Sun- and Jupiter-ruled lagnas, Rahu for Saturn-ruled) is also said to give inverted results under the retro effect, which is why nodes get a retrograde score too. This contradicts the yogakaraka rule above `(?)`. `[1:05:13]`
- 3rd inverted: siblings take long to settle; consulting and communication do well. `[0:34:41]`
- 7th inverted: marriage and relationships suffer; public dealings, consultancy and outside people are strong. `[0:21:26]`
- 10th inverted: job, authority and responsibility come without satisfaction (wrong job, wrong position). `[1:05:13]`
- 11th inverted: easy gains, but friends may be missing (captions also mention brotherly relationships `(?)`). `[1:06:13]`
- 12th inverted: can bring extreme wealth, with unknown fears, worry about losses and psychological strain. `[1:06:13]`
- Low scores for lagna-malefics are good: inverted, they give yoga (money, job). If they are strong instead, the relationships balance. `[1:32:39]`
- If something must be lost, it should be the malefics; lost benefics take everything with them. `[1:33:39]`
- Sun and Moon lost as lagna-malefics still give yoga in their dashas, though their bhavas are gone. `[1:34:42]`

## Dasha delivery

- Outside Rule 4, a dasha lord delivers through its own star lord. Use the dasha lord's own final score, which already places it 25% in its own house and 75% in its star lord's house. Don't swap in the star lord's own score, and don't re-judge the star lord's goodness or retrogression at this step. `[0:51:02]`
- Calculator (dasha analysis) was written to take the dasha lord at 25% plus the star lord's own Light, Performance and Resource at 75%. A good star lord would then mask the dasha lord's retrograde fault, so this may be an error to fix `(?)`. `[0:53:06]`
- Rule 4: if exactly one planet sits in the dasha lord's nakshatras, use that planet's score and houses (Moon dasha delivered by Jupiter in Shravana, not the Moon's own score). The occupant takes the star lord's 75% share; the dasha lord with its own star lord keeps 25%. `[0:45:54]`
- If that single occupant is debilitated, ignore it and follow the dasha lord's own star lord (Moon dasha follows Ketu, not a debilitated Jupiter). Calculator applies this correctly, though its written flow doesn't list the rule. `[0:46:55]`
- Stated as provisional: a dasha lord with a retrograde fault puts its inverted results on the house it occupies (Sun in the 7th: the 7th), not on its star lord's house. The star lord's house (the 5th) still gets its share through the 75%; without the fault the Sun would simply act as if in the 5th. `[0:54:06]`
- The dasha lord itself must be sound; if it isn't, results come hard. `[0:55:07]`
- A dasha acts on the house its lord occupies, its star lord's house, and houses tied to the star lord by conjunction or aspect. Sun dasha: the 7th, the 5th, the 6th lord joined with the star lord, the 2nd lord aspecting it. `[0:45:54]`
- A planet with no marks in Light, Performance and Resource gives neither bhava nor yoga; its dasha simply doesn't work. `[1:44:49]`
- A dasha can hit either house its lord owns (Jupiter: 7th and 10th for Gemini) when the period runs badly. `[1:43:47]`
- Dual lordship: a planet acts for whichever of its two houses is unoccupied. For Gemini with planets in the 6th, Mars acts as 11th lord, so its dasha runs through the 11th and the 5th where it sits. `[1:35:42]`
- A lagna-malefic that is inverted and linked to the 11th gives yoga strongly and fast in its dasha (Mars for Gemini). `[1:37:44]`
- The lagna-nakshatra lord's dasha turns the life (Gemini lagna in Mrigashira: Mars dasha). `[1:37:44]`
- The lagna-nakshatra lord weighs more than the Moon sign, but the Moon's nakshatra also matters a lot. `[2:02:08]`
- The dasha running at birth shows the family background: a good one means birth into a good family and position, and lays the foundation. `[1:35:42]`
- Two planets tied by a star (Mars in Rahu's star) give yoga to the same degree, both full or both half. Each dasha has a first and a second part (pattern still to come). `[1:46:50]`
- Planets sharing a sign and linked by stars (Saturn in its own star, Venus in Mercury's star, all in the 6th) help each other; any of their dashas delivers that sign's yoga. `[1:45:49]`
- Everyone runs the same nine dashas, and star lords often signify the 10th or 11th for everyone. The planet and house scores decide how much each delivers; score first to avoid overestimating. `[1:07:14]`

## Star lord and yogakaraka

- Key rule: a planet whose star lord is the lagna's yogakaraka, or is joined with it, gives good results; otherwise not. `[2:06:15]`
- For Gemini, Venus is the special yogakaraka: 5th lord and a natural benefic, and a natural benefic owning a trine gives strong yogakarakatva. Saturn, the 9th lord, is a natural malefic and counts for less. `[1:57:02]`
- When a strong yogakaraka leads a sign holding several planets, every dasha whose star-lord chain reaches that sign works like the yogakaraka's own dasha, overriding apparent doshas there (guru-chandala, Mars–Rahu). Example: Venus in its own sign in the 5th with Jupiter, Rahu and Mars; the Sun in Chitra follows Mars, Mars follows Venus, so the Sun dasha is good. Without Venus, both the Mars and the Sun dashas fail. `[2:07:17]`
- If the yogakaraka star lord is debilitated, see who controls its sign. An exalted co-tenant overrides (Mercury exalted with a debilitated Venus in Virgo: good). A malefic leader (Mars in its own star) with the yogakaraka debilitated elsewhere gives torment and no peace. `[2:10:21]`
- For results to flourish, a Pancha Mahapurusha or vipareeta raja yoga must form and the yogakaraka must be tied to it. `[2:14:23]`
- One strong factor can hold a weak chart: a well-placed lagna lord in the 10th, or a vipareeta raja yoga through one planet, keeps the rest average rather than worst. Look for Pancha Mahapurusha (e.g. Bhadra) and vipareeta yogas. `[1:33:39]`
- Birth star: its lord decides, not the star itself. Check who controls that lord and what yoga it forms with kendra and trine lords. Born in Venus's star with Venus debilitated beside Rahu: mental worry and emotional obstacles. `[1:57:02]`

## Leading planet and influence

- When a sign holds several planets, the strongest one leads and influences the rest. A planet in its own nakshatra leads. `[1:19:25]`
- An exalted planet leads (the Sun exalted in Aries leads Mercury, Venus and Rahu there). `[1:51:57]`
- Digbala by connection also gives the lead: Jupiter joined with the lagna lord Mercury (Gemini) gets digbala and leads. `[1:47:51]`
- If neither planet has its digbala connection (the Moon needs a 4th-house link and Rahu a 7th-house link, checked through their star lords), the sequence of planets decides `(?)`: Rahu led over the Moon. `[1:10:18]`
- A debilitated leader has no strength: with Mercury beside it in Capricorn, Mercury leads instead. Alone and unafflicted in the follower's own sign (Jupiter in Capricorn for Saturn), it still balances somewhat. `[1:49:55]`
- A planet follows the leader of the sign where its star lord sits. Saturn in Rahu's star, with Rahu beside an exalted Sun: the Sun gives Saturn's results, and Rahu plays a lesser part. `[1:54:01]`
- In a planet's own dasha, look at its own star lord first, not at the leader of its own sign. Rahu sits beside the exalted Sun, but its star lord Venus is debilitated in the 4th, so Rahu's dasha falls short. `[1:55:01]`
- The leader controls but doesn't replace. The follower must itself be sound; give only some marks for the leader's influence. `[1:23:30]`
- Score the overriding influence inside the star-lord portion of the planet that follows. The star lord's own lordships don't matter, but it must have strength (not debilitated). `[1:27:32]`
- An afflicted leader (Jupiter with Rahu) gives its followers complicated guru-chandala results. `[1:49:55]`
- Jupiter in the 3rd carries a dosha (guru-tritiya; captions also say "Bhrigu shatka" `(?)`) and is a lagna-malefic for Gemini. Saturn in Mercury's star, with Mercury under that Jupiter's lead, underperforms in its dasha. `[1:48:54]`
- An exalted leader lets the follower override the sign's affliction (Rahu there) and take on the exaltation: results mostly come, with small problems that get solved. `[1:52:59]`
- Count the overriding planets in the chart: planets under good leaders deliver in almost any dasha. `[1:45:49]`
- Before trusting an exalted leader, check its Light: retrogression (Mercury running ahead of the Sun may be retrograde) and node conjunctions. `[2:11:21]`

## Timing: dasha and progression

- Progressions and transits come in only at the dasha step. Progressions are enough; transits are needed only for timing within about a month. `[1:00:10]`
- A natal weakness (e.g. a weak 7th) shows only when timing turns negative, i.e. when a bad dasha runs. `[1:43:47]`
- Marriage depends on the dasha running at marriageable age. Good dasha lords (Mars linked to the 11th, Rahu following a good Venus) save a weak 7th. The dasha of the 7th lord (Jupiter) in a difficult period with a bad progression brings trouble with the spouse. `[1:41:46]`

## Light

- Light is one score for the planet and its star lord together, not split into shares. `[0:08:15]`
- The retrograde fault comes from the planet's own retrogression, a node sharing its nakshatra, or a star lord that carries the fault (a node, or a star lord joined with a node). Debilitation has nothing to do with Light. `[1:09:16]`
- The Sun in Ketu's star (Moola) took the fault; captions say the Sun "shares a nakshatra with Rahu" `(?)`. `[0:09:16]`
- With the fault and no exception, Light is 35 at most (Sun with its star lord Ketu in the 5th). It can't be raised without an exception. `[0:10:17]`
- Exception: star lord in the 11th gives Light 80. Examples: Mars in Rahu's star with Rahu in the 11th; Jupiter in the Moon's star with the Moon beside Rahu in the 11th bhava. `[0:28:32]`
- An exalted planet, one in its own house, or one in parivartana gets the Light exception directly, whatever its star lord. `[0:12:20]`
- Saturn in its own star, with its sign lord Mars under the retro effect, was given Light 60 `(?)` (reason not stated; matches the 60% minimum guarantee). `[1:19:25]`
- A planet and star lord both free of the fault get 100 (Venus). `[1:25:31]`
- Any planet linked to Rahu or Ketu loses marks from the retro effect. The Sun and Moon never turn retrograde, so their fault comes only from the nodes (conjunction, or sitting in a node's star); it mostly stays fixed and the score stays low. `[0:38:48]`

## Performance

- Performance = planet 25% + star-lord portion 75%, out of 100. Each part may be scored out of 100 and scaled. `[0:09:16]`
- The planet part judges its actual house. The star-lord portion judges the planet as if it sat in its star lord's house and sign: the Sun in Ketu's star, with Ketu in Libra, is judged as a Sun in the 5th, debilitated. The star lord's own nature and further links come later. `[0:11:19]`
- Performance is judged on position. Digbala belongs to Resource; the star lord's aspects and company add only a mark or two. `[0:39:48]`
- Aspects and conjunctions are worth only 1–2 marks. In Performance, note benefic or malefic company and aspects on the star lord. In Resource, note conjunction with an exalted planet, an exalted planet's star `(?)` and benefic aspects. Position and basic dignities are mostly enough. `[1:01:10]`
- The star-lord portion also depends on how the planet gets on with its star lord and on the star lord's company. It gets more through a lagna yogakaraka or a friend, less through Rahu or an enemy (the Sun following Saturn), and little when the star lord is debilitated or sits with the Moon and Rahu. `[0:31:36]`
- Star lord in the 11th: the star-lord portion gets about 75%, i.e. 56 of 75. `[0:29:32]`
- Judge what the planet itself gains from the placement, not the star lord's lordships. For Venus following Mercury, Mercury's ownership of two kendras is irrelevant; Venus gets the 6th. `[1:26:32]`
- Malefics, Rahu and Ketu included, score high only in the upachaya houses (stated as 3, 6, 11). Rahu in the 8th can't get 70–80. `[2:05:13]`
- Rahu is an air planet: better in fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) and air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), worst in Pisces, then the water signs Scorpio and Cancer. By house, Rahu in the 10th is better; combine all this into the position mark. `[2:05:13]`
- The sign itself counts for little in Performance (Rahu gains nothing for sitting in Saturn's sign). `[2:19:29]`
- A natural malefic in the 6th is excellently placed (Saturn: 23 of 25). `[1:19:25]`
- A non-yogakaraka in the 8th (dusthana) gets about 15 of 25. `[1:10:18]`
- The Sun in the 5th carries a dosha (captions "arkastu panchamah", "balarishta" `(?)`), lower still if debilitated there. The 5th being auspicious keeps it middling (50 of 75). `[0:11:19]`
- Venus in the 6th carries Bhrigu shatka dosha, so low marks (15 of 25). A 12th lord in the 6th is acceptable: add about 5. `[1:25:31]`
- Jupiter whose star lord sits with Rahu acts like guru-chandala yoga in that house (linked to the 11th: 45 of 75). `[1:10:18]`
- A vipareeta raja yoga (8th and 12th lords together in the 6th) raises the star-lord portion despite gandanta (Venus: 55 of 75). `[1:29:35]`

## Resource

- Resource = planet 60% + star lord 40%. `[0:12:20]`
- Resource is dignity: exaltation, debilitation, friend's or own sign, moolatrikona, uttama drekkana, gandanta, pushkara navamsha, digbala. Lordship and position belong to Performance. `[0:17:24]`
- The planet part is the planet's own dignity where it actually sits. The star-lord part is the star lord's own dignity, judged on its own (unlike Performance, the planet isn't moved into the star lord's sign). `[0:14:22]`
- Judge dignity on the planet first; the star lord's dignity helps, or stands in when the planet has none. An exalted planet does well even from the 8th, whatever its star lord (Jupiter exalted in the 8th for Sagittarius lagna). `[0:13:20]`
- Planet and star lord both debilitated, with no overriding planet: trouble. `[0:13:20]`
- Digbala can come by connection: the Sun gets it only with a link to the 10th lord (Jupiter for Gemini) through its star lord. `[0:13:20]`
- Baseline with no problem: 30 of 60 (50%); raise or lower from there. Exalted: about 55, more at the exact exaltation degree, in moolatrikona or own sign. `[0:17:24]`
- Neither exalted nor debilitated: minimum guarantee (Mars 35 of 60, since its star lord links it to its own sign). `[0:29:32]`
- A planet in a gandanta pada has no Resource; with Rahu too, it falls below pass marks (Moon in Ashwini pada 1: 25 of 60, and that only because it is in the 11th). `[0:42:53]`
- Star-lord part: digbala, own-nakshatra position or conjunction with an exalted planet raise it; bad company lowers it. `[0:43:53]`
- Own nakshatra is strong dignity (Saturn in Anuradha: 40–50 of 60). `[1:20:25]`
- A debilitated planet gets very little (Jupiter: 25 of 60). `[1:11:19]`
- Rahu as a star lord can't be trusted (greedy; may win or lose), so star-lord marks stay low. The Moon with Rahu can bring health problems. `[0:36:45]`
- Deep exaltation degree, uttama drekkana and vargottama each add points. `[2:11:21]`
- Being in an enemy's sign carries very little weight. `[1:29:35]`

## Capacity

- Capacity = Light/100 × (0.6 × Performance + 0.4 × Resource); multiply by Light before combining. Checks: Jupiter 0.8 × (0.6 × 60 + 0.4 × 45) = 43.2 exactly; Mars ≈ 52; Saturn ≈ 44. `[0:18:25]`
- Poor Light drags everything down. The Sun, with Performance 65 and Light 35, gets 22.75 × 0.6 = 13.65 from Performance and about 21 in all (later cited as 22 `(?)`); with Light 100 it would be average. Raising Resource then changes little. `[0:19:25]`

## Houses and significations

- Sun and Moon are father and mother. When both score very low, parental support is weak and the native must be self-made. It is milder when they are lagna-malefics (they then give yoga), and far worse if they own the 4th or 9th. `[0:43:53]`
- When Sun and Moon are spoiled, their dashas bring the parents' health troubles. `[1:43:47]`
- A strong 6th protects health (a lost 6th means ill health) and gives gains and fighting spirit. A 6th lord whose fault is cancelled by an exception is strong, and the native stays healthy. `[1:21:27]`
- A debilitated 10th lord in the 8th (Jupiter for Gemini) gives twisted results and sudden, unexpected spikes in marriage and career; a dasha delivered through it activates the 8th. `[0:46:55]`
- Delay is signified mainly by Saturn, also Ketu. Next comes Rahu, which acts like Saturn ("Shanivat Rahu"), though Rahu is also sudden. `[0:56:07]`
- The Sun joined with Rahu gives knowledge beyond need and ego problems. `[1:38:44]`
- Rahu brings big expectations (Mars linked to Rahu). `[1:31:37]`
- The Moon signifies milk (a Moon–Rahu transit: trouble over milk). Mars, Venus and Rahu together point to counterfeit currency and violence. `[2:15:25]`
- Rare artistic talent shows through Mercury and Ketu `(?)`. `[2:17:28]`
- Gandanta delays results, but they come. `[1:29:35]`

## Placements and yogas

It is not in the automatic scoring; use it rarely, as a manual third-level check. Here it helped a weak 7th hold. `[0:20:26]`
- Nakshatra exchange (Mars in Rahu's star, Rahu in Mars's star) is good when the sign lords involved are good. `[2:04:11]`

## Bhava scoring

- Judge a bhava from three planets: its strongest occupant, the bhava lord and the bhava karaka (e.g., Jupiter for the 5th). Read each one through its star lord and the star lord's links. `[0:03:03]`
- Weights: strongest occupant 60%, bhava lord 20%, karaka 20%. Compute Light, Performance and Resource for each of the three; their weighted capacity is the bhava's strength. `[0:51:42]`
- With no planet in the bhava, the occupant's 60 goes to the bhava lord, which then carries 80%. `[1:01:00]`
- The 60/20/20 split is a working choice; another split may be used if it works in practice. `[0:05:09]`
- A low weight does not keep a planet quiet. When its period runs, a weak karaka (only 20%) can still bring miscarriage, blocked progress or other suffering, even with a good 5th occupant. `[0:05:09]`
- A bad occupant with a good lord and karaka may give results for a while, then strikes when its dasha or bhukti becomes relevant. In a good period this shows as distance (e.g., the child lives far away); in a bad one, as health trouble. `[0:06:10]`
- The bhava a planet occupies (Placidus cusps from the lagna) matters more than its sign. `[1:02:00]`
- A planet a few degrees short of the cusp still counts as that house's occupant (captions: 19° against 22°). When a single planet is there, take it. `(?)` `[1:38:54]`
- Each link of the deliverer to a dusthana adds another negative. The bhava still works, but its problems pile up. `[1:59:12]`
- The lord and the karaka are evaluated the same way as the occupant. `[1:40:54]`
- A bhava is what can be touched and experienced; intangible matters are treated as yoga. `[0:50:41]`
- When a retrogression takes effect, bhava and yoga results turn opposite: the tangible (bhava) result is lost while the yoga still yields `(?)`. `[1:05:11]`
- The karaka-in-its-own-bhava check and bhavat-bhavam are not used. `[1:07:13]`

## Who runs a bhava

- If the house has occupants, take the strongest one and score it through its star lord. That score fills the occupant's 60. `[1:09:18]`
- A lone occupant without Light (e.g., Rahu) spoils the bhava. A stronger second occupant, such as the lagna lord in its own star, becomes the leading planet and protects the bhava; its sign lord then decides the result. `[2:03:16]`
- With no occupant, check the house lord in this order: own star; parivartana with its star lord; exaltation or own sign; several planets in its stars; a single planet in its stars. The first case that applies decides. This holds for any house. `[1:26:42]`
- Lord in its own star: its sign lord takes the star lord's role and gives the results. `[1:10:18]`
- A lord in its own star is safe even in gandanta; the result comes through its sign lord (e.g., a sign lord in the 11th gives gains). `[1:23:34]`
- A lord in its own star is protected even when retrograde; the result then depends on its sign lord. `[2:09:26]`
- Lord in parivartana with its star lord (e.g., a star exchange): read it from its partner's position, and that sign's lord decides. For example, Venus in the Moon's star with the Moon in Venus's star in Leo is read from Leo, so the Sun decides. `[1:11:19]`
- After a parivartana, the stronger of the two sign lords (original and exchanged) leads, and the other still colours the result `(?)`. `[1:33:50]`
- Lord exalted or in its own sign: it runs the bhava directly through its own star lord (e.g., Taurus lagna, empty 5th, Mercury in Gemini). `[1:11:19]`
- Several planets in the lord's stars: ignore them; the lord and its own star lord decide. `[1:13:20]`
- In that case even a debilitated lord is fine, because the result rides on its star lord's placement. Star lord in the 9th: children are good. Star lord in the 8th: problems such as miscarriage. `[1:22:34]`
- Exactly one planet in the lord's stars: that planet runs the bhava, even when the lord itself is strong. If that planet is retrograde, the bhava suffers. `[1:19:30]`
- The lord's own strength is scored in its own 20% slot; it does not decide who runs the bhava. `[1:21:32]`
- Otherwise, the lord's original star lord applies (default). `[1:14:21]`

## Lagna as booster

- The lagna, lagna lord and lagna-nakshatra lord act as a booster that protects every bhava; all 12 bhavas sit inside the lagna. While they are good, even a weak bhava is shielded. `[0:52:45]`
- A strong lagna gives the other bhavas a 5–10% boost. A weak lagna keeps the other bhavas from delivering fully. If the lagna, the lagna-nakshatra lord, the Sun and the Moon are all poor, nothing in the chart works. `[1:04:10]`
- "Strong" has no percentage threshold; do not use a 50% cut-off. A strong lagna can lift a weak bhava as an exception, e.g., from 30% to about 50%, so children still come despite an issue. That lift is larger than the 5–10% boost `(?)`. `[1:05:11]`
- A good lagna bhava needs a good lagna-nakshatra lord, the lagna in an uttama drekkana, a good lagna lord, and any strong planet in the lagna in good condition with good associations. `[1:05:11]`
- A strong lagna plus a Pancha Mahapurusha yoga or exaltations lifts the whole standard of life (e.g., ten times the income). `[1:05:11]`

## Dasha flow and timing

- Sequence: planet strength (capacity), then bhava analysis (strongest and weakest areas of life), then dasha analysis (when it happens). `[0:00:00]`
- Bhava analysis and dasha analysis follow the same flow and the same rules. For a dasha, the dasha lord takes the place of the house lord. `[0:48:35]`
- A single planet in the dasha lord's stars delivers that dasha. In Rahu's dasha, Venus alone in Rahu's star gives the results; in Venus's dasha, look to its star lord Rahu. `[2:04:18]`
- In Saturn's dasha, Rahu sitting in Saturn's star brings its own retrogression and Light condition into play. `[2:05:19]`
- Time natal defects with the dasha. First estimate what problems the chart can give, then find when they come. In a good mahadasha the problem comes small and gets solved; in a bad period the native gets stuck. `[0:53:47]`
- A problem shows fully only when the relevant dasha runs. Otherwise it takes another form (e.g., the child lives far away). No problem is lifelong; everything is time-bound. `[1:29:43]`

## Progression and the dynamic natal chart

- Capacity is not fixed. When a planet turns retrograde or direct in progression, recompute its Light and capacity from the dynamic chart. `[0:32:46]`
- A natal-retrograde planet is judged at the time of its dasha, not at birth. If it has turned direct by then, treat it as direct; with no defect at that time, take good results. `[1:49:04]`
- If the planet running a bhava is retrograde only in childhood and turns direct later, the damage stays in that window. For the 5th, the native's own childhood suffers (e.g., hostel life away from parents), not his later children. `[1:20:31]`
- Re-check who runs a bhava at the time of the event. A planet can move back retrograde into the lord's star and stay there for years (a Venus retrogression lasts about 42 days, i.e., years in progression `(?)`). If the native is trying for children then, expect complications. `[1:21:32]`
- If the problem planet has left the lord's star by the time of the event, the problem is gone. Slow planets such as Saturn hold these states for a long time. `[1:28:42]`

## Light, retrogression and exceptions

- Light defect: the planet is retrograde or linked with Rahu/Ketu. Both together cancel (minus × minus). `[0:13:15]`
- With no retrogression and no Rahu/Ketu link, Light is 100%. `[0:37:06]`
- The Rahu/Ketu link comes from sharing stars with them. For example, the Moon in Swati with Ketu in Shatabhisha (both Rahu stars) activates the Light algorithm. This is how the Sun and Moon, which never retrograde, lose Light `(?)`. `[1:52:05]`
- A planet does not lose Light because its star lord (e.g., Rahu) lacks Light; only its own link with Rahu/Ketu counts. `[2:05:19]`
- A Light-defective star lord in the 10th (an exception house) is partly excused. The result is not the worst, but some issue remains (e.g., in the support system); the 11th would be better. `[1:31:46]`
- A planet that has lost Light gives extremes. Sometimes it gives nothing; when its dasha works it gives lavishly, and when it gives trouble there is no recovery. With Light intact it acts normally. Whether the extreme is good or bad is judged by the time. `[2:01:14]`
- The extreme effect appears only when the dasha flow activates the planet. `[2:04:18]`
- If the planet passes the first-level Light check, it can work normally; otherwise the real, extreme activation starts. `[2:06:21]`
- Strong positional strength can keep a Light defect from ever acting; for example, Rahu in the 2nd delivers directly in its period `(?)`. `[2:07:23]`
- Rahu and Ketu have no lagna-based benefic or malefic status, so apply the rules to them normally. When they turn retrograde in progression, expect extreme, out-of-the-ordinary results `(?)`. `[2:02:15]`

## Planet capacity (Light, Performance, Resource)

- Whether a planet can deliver depends on three things, Light, Performance and Resource, not on exaltation or debilitation alone. `[0:36:04]`
- Order: check Light first, then score the star lord's position (Performance), then add dignity (Resource). Without Light the other marks are worthless. `[0:44:25]`
- Capacity = Performance × Light/100 + Resource × Light/100. Performance is out of 60, Resource out of 40, and Light is a 0–100% multiplier. `[0:11:13]`
- A planet gives only about 20–25% of its results from its own placement and 75–80% through its star lord (the 25/75 rule). `[0:13:15]`
- Finer split: planet 20, star lord 50, and the strongest planet in the star lord's sign (the planet controlling the star lord) 30. The worked calculation used the plain 25/75 split `(?)`. `[0:12:15]`
- Position marks count from the lagna. Rough values in the captions: trikonas about 75–90, the 2nd and 11th good, a kendra such as the 10th up to 100, a dusthana such as the 8th about 25 `(?)`. `[0:37:06]`
- A planet in a dusthana, or debilitated, still gives excellent results with a good star lord: planet in the 8th (25) with its star lord in the 10th (100) scores 25 × 25% + 100 × 75% = 81.25. `[0:37:06]`
- Worked example: Light 60, position score 80, Resource 80%. Performance = 80 × 60/100 = 48, then × 60% Light = 28.8. Resource = 80 × 60% Light = 48, then × 40/100 = 19.2. Capacity = 48. (The spoken running totals were off; these figures check.) `[0:28:38]`
- Judge the star lord first by position (links to good houses). Yogakaraka status and dignity (Resource) come second. `[1:23:34]`
- Resource comes from dignity: own sign, exaltation, dig bala (captions: 60–100% `(?)`), vargottama, uttama drekkana. `[0:38:09]`
- Sitting in a yogakaraka's star connects a planet to the yogakaraka and raises its Resource. `[0:28:38]`
- The strongest planet in a sign is picked by dignity, exaltation first, then own sign; the rest of the order is garbled `(?)`. `[0:38:09]`
- Vargottama and similar boosts must count negatively for a debilitated planet. Calculator shows them as added strength. `[0:23:32]`
- Do not go on to the star lord's star lord (the old jeeva/sharira idea). It carries only about 10%, cannot rescue a bad star lord, and is worth a look only when a prediction is failing. `[0:08:11]`
- For the strongest planet in the star lord's sign, check only its Light (retrogression, Rahu/Ketu) and go no deeper. `[0:23:32]`
- The planet, its star lord and the strongest planet in the star lord's sign should be friends; then results are good. Even so, a dusthana placement (poor position score) blocks the results. `[0:23:32]`
- A weak planet with a good star lord still largely delivers; with both good it delivers fully. The reverse reduces the result `(?)`. `[0:22:30]`
- Good results need no Light defect and a star lord in good houses, linked to yogakarakas or friends. Dignities of the star lord (own sign, exaltation, moolatrikona, vargottama, uttama drekkana, parivartana, pushkara navamsa, star exchange, own star, dig bala) add more. `[0:19:26]`

## Houses and significations

- Kendras and trikonas are always good houses, and planets in them mostly give favourable results (captions: 1, 4, 7, 10 and 5, 9 `(?)`, with 10 and 11). Planets in dusthanas rarely give good results easily; sometimes, as in viparita raja yoga, they give good results after trouble. `[0:13:15]`
- A planet in a dusthana in its own sign or exaltation is an accepted exception (e.g., for Aries lagna, the 12th lord Jupiter in Pisces). `[0:14:21]`
- The 3rd, being 8th from the 8th, gives results only through effort. `[0:15:21]`
- The 11th gives gains; where it is also the badhaka (e.g., Aries lagna), the gains come after hardship. `[1:33:50]`
- The lordship of the planet leading a bhava sets its theme. A 6th lord leading the 2nd brings family-health trouble; if a yogakaraka leads instead, the problem shrinks to some family-health responsibility. `[1:34:52]`
- Parivartana protects somewhat, e.g., against Ketu afflicting the lord. `[1:35:52]`
- Parivartana does not remove a defect but lets the matter happen in some form, e.g., children with complications or a marriage with problems. `[1:57:10]`
- The lagna stands for own strength, self-confidence and self-effort. A 3rd bhava whose deliverer links to the lagna (e.g., through the lagna lord as star lord) is good: the native achieves through his own effort and communicates well. The 3rd (vikrama) also covers the support ecosystem. `[1:37:52]`
- The 6th is disease. When the 6th's deliverer links to the 5th (12th from the 6th), the 6th is negated: illness comes and goes. The 6th needs such relief, or it troubles. The 6th lord in the 6th gives immunity. `[1:39:54]`
- For the 7th, a deliverer tied to the 6th lord in the 11th (plus an 8th-lord link) lowers the rating: struggle with the spouse and less harmony. `[1:56:08]`
- The Moon with Ketu brings detachment; with Rahu, worries. A strong Moon may change this `(?)`. `[1:59:12]`
- Mercury leading alongside Rahu in the 2nd favours speculative gains. A good Jupiter keeps it ethical (Jupiter in the 5th is best); otherwise it turns into real gambling. `[2:08:24]`

## Aspects

- Squares (kendra aspects) are harsh and bring challenges; trines and sextiles let things pass smoothly, even an accident. When transits in certain nakshatras form squares to the natal Sun or Moon, take care (falls, accidents, anxiety). `[2:00:12]`
- The reverse (apasavya) 5th aspect of Rahu and Ketu is not used. `[1:50:05]`

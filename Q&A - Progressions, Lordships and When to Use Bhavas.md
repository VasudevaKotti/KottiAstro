## Bhava vs house

- A bhava starts at its cusp and runs until the next cusp begins. `[0:32:34]`
- The boundary allowance is about 3°. A planet deeper inside a house than that belongs to that house, even allowing for small chart differences. `[0:14:15]`
- A planet in its own sign needs no house/bhava split: the whole house is its own and it pulls the house to itself. Own-sign Mars in the 7th house but the 6th bhava is read in the 7th for its mahadasha (its star lord in the 7th bhava reinforces this); the bhava still links but does not work against it. `[0:33:35]`
- An occupied house is judged by its occupant under the occupant rules, including the occupant's own retrogression. Only an unoccupied house is led by its lord, so a retrograde lord's inverted result falls only on its unoccupied houses (the lord of 3 and 6 inverted the 6th, not the 3rd, which held the Moon). No house gets priority; go by occupancy. `(?)` That lord was in parivartana, which at 0:05:04 was said to cancel its retrogression, yet its empty 6th still took the inversion. `[0:25:28]`
- Vedic aspects are counted in signs from the sign the planet occupies; bhavas play no part. `[0:36:38]`

## Star lord and dasha delivery

- To read any planet, go to its star lord: where the star lord sits, which planets it is with, what lordships they hold, and which planets aspect them. `[0:34:36]`
- In a mahadasha the dasha lord's results come through its star lord (Mars's through Ketu). Only when R4 applies does the planet in the dasha lord's star deliver instead. `[0:34:36]`
- R4: when one planet alone sits in the dasha lord's star, that planet delivers the dasha from its own house (the Moon's dasha came through the Sun in the 8th). `[0:13:14]`
- A house's event does not need the house's occupant to run as dasha or bhukti lord. It is enough for the running period's chain to reach the house, for example through the period lord's star lord and the planets joined with it. `[0:14:15]`
- A bhukti comes alive when the dasha lord sits in the bhukti lord's star. Follow the chain: the bhukti lord's house, its star lord's house, the planets joined with that star lord (a karaka there helps, e.g. Jupiter for children), and their aspects. If it reaches the relevant houses (1, 5, 9 for children), the period can deliver; the dasha pattern matters. `[0:15:15]`

## Retrograde inversion and exceptions

- A planet in parivartana: ignore its own retrogression. It follows the planet the exchange links it to and is as good as that planet (Jupiter followed Venus: Venus good, Jupiter good). `(?)` At 0:30:33 the exchange is described as Jupiter with Rahu; how it leads to Venus is not stated. `[0:05:04]`
- Parivartana also cancels a functional malefic's malefic nature: Jupiter as 3rd and 6th lord gives the results Venus would give. `[0:06:05]`
- Parivartana still leaves a residue. Such a retrograde planet scored 80 (captions: "life score", probably Light `(?)`), and the remaining 20% inverted share shows as minor trouble (delays, immunity or gynaecological problems) rather than denial. `[0:07:06]`
- A retrograde planet whose star lord sits on the lagna has the exception (Saturn in the star of Rahu on the lagna). A retrograde 5th lord brings challenges for children from the time it turns retrograde; with the exception, children still come when the relevant period arrives. `[0:05:04]`
- Exceptions protect only the native. Relatives judged from their houses (e.g. parents) do not share the native's exceptions, so they can suffer while the native stays unaffected. `[0:20:20]`
- In the retrograde (Light) score, a retrograde planet gets 80 with an exception and 60 with "strength" (own star, own sign or exaltation), which acts as a minimum guarantee. Own star alone counts as strength and gets 60% (calculator rule R6); this belongs to the retrograde score only. `[0:40:44]`
- The exception check looks at the star lord's placement in both house and bhava, weighted house 25 / bhava 75. A star lord in the 12th house but the 11th bhava earns only the bhava share, giving values like 67.5 or 68. `(?)` With 80 as the exception value the bhava share alone would be 60; 67.5 is 75% of 90. `[0:37:40]`

## Dasha timing

- Judge the time first (good or bad), then attach the combinations to it. Reading planets and combinations without judging time is the usual error. `[0:08:07]`
- A planet's inverted results (retrograde or functional malefic) work only while its own dasha or bhukti runs; outside that time set them aside. For each period use what was defined for that dasha lord and bhukti lord at that time. `[0:09:09]`
- Light is only a hint. Don't forecast from the rise and fall of a planet's Light; judge the situation at the time being predicted. `[0:03:02]`
- A drop in Light produces an event only when the relevant dasha-bhukti triggers it at the same time. Without that timing, a Light drop may cause no loss at all. `[0:04:02]`
- A dosha lies in the timing, not in the combination: judge the dasha and check it against the progression. The combination carried only about 20% `(?)` (unclear whether this is a general weight or this chart's share). `[0:06:05]`
- Keep predictions in step with the native's age and the running timeline. Don't jump to a later mahadasha because its lord looks promising (e.g. a well-placed 2nd lord for family growth). `[0:23:25]`

## When to use bhavas

- Bhavas (house significations) are needed only for a strong positive or negative prediction. Otherwise predict from the quality of the time: a good period can deliver an event even without a direct link to the relevant house. `[0:17:17]`
- Don't apply fixed house formulas (for job, marriage and so on) mechanically; what works depends on the chart. Where viparita raja yoga drives the chart (common in sports charts), the 6th delivers and the trines don't, and activation of the 5th brought failure. `[0:18:18]`
- Any planet can give any result; don't confine a planet to its natural significations (Venus is not only marriage). The lagna connects to all 12 bhavas, so a good lagna-lord period can deliver whatever the native pursues; good time plus good combination means it happens with effort. `[0:19:20]`

## Progression (dynamic natal chart)

- Damage done while a planet was retrograde is not undone when it turns direct (a retrograde 9th-house planet's losses to the father stay lost). The direct phase only allows fresh building, and only if the time is then favourable; a planet with strength (here own sign) leaves a minimum guarantee to restart from. `[0:02:00]`
- To explain a change in the native (such as a change of mind), look back along the progression at the aspects formed then, and at the spouse's chart where relevant. A progressed square between the Sun and the 10th cusp brought frustration and a change of decision. `[0:22:24]`
- In a progressed aspect, ignore the house the progressed planet is passing through (a progressed Sun moving through the 9th is not a 9th-house factor). Read only the aspect and the natal placement of the planet aspected (natal Saturn in the 5th makes it a 5th-house matter). `[0:27:30]`
- Lordships never change in progression; always take them from the natal chart. `[0:27:30]`
- An aspect involving a planet that is retrograde at that time is a reversal: even a trine turns negative. The negativity comes from the reversal, not from the other planet's lordship (the Sun being badhaka lord). `[0:28:31]`
- Progressions show the kind of experience (what sort of difficulty or ease) and stay generic. The concrete result comes from the dasha-antardasha, so interpret mainly through the dasha. `[0:28:31]`
- Check the orb, about 1.2–1.5°. A wide, approaching aspect is weak; predict strongly only near exactness. `[0:29:32]`
- Aspects use the progressed planet's progressed degree, retrograde or not. `[0:29:32]`
- A planet that turned retrograde in progression regains its exemption only when it turns direct again; moving back past its natal degree changes nothing. `[0:29:32]`

## Nodes

- Nodes own no houses. Read a normal planet by position plus lordships; read a node by its position, add the lordships of any lord joined with it, and read it as a pair with the planets that follow it (Venus–Rahu, Saturn–Rahu). A node standing alone is read by position only. `[0:31:34]`
- Planets that follow a node take on the node's combination. With Rahu in exchange with the 6th lord, its followers act like the 6th lord: ambitious, and frustrated when things don't go their way. `[0:30:33]`
- A node leads on its own. A planet joined with it does not become the leader, but its connection still works. `[0:31:34]`
- When a planet follows a node, the node's own score still matters: both the node and the planet joined with it must be good for the result to work well. `(?)` Garbled exchange. `[0:11:11]`
- The nodes are not treated as retrograde when acting as star lords (Rahu as a star lord was called "not retro"). `(?)` Implied, not stated as a rule. `[0:16:15]`

## Houses, yogas and significations

- Houses 1, 5 and 9 operating together signify luck. `[0:16:15]`
- When the santana dosha is not heavy, predict delay, not denial. If neither partner has a medical problem, children come once the periods fall right. `[0:05:04]`
- A functional malefic (3rd and 6th lord) on the lagna whose malefic side is cancelled by parivartana makes the native ambitious but not rude, and polite in speech. `[0:07:06]`
- Saturn–Rahu linked to the lagna gives unconventional thinking: no attachment to a set method, fine with a shortcut as long as the work gets done. `[0:31:34]`
- The badhaka lord acts as badhaka wherever it sits `(?)`. Placed in the 8th it is good in itself, but when it delivers another planet's dasha (R4 route) the result can be twisted and mixed. `[0:13:14]`
- Whether a relative's situation recovers (e.g. parents reuniting) depends on the relatives' own charts. `[0:21:22]`

## Other methods

- Uttama drekkana `(?)`: movable signs 1–10°, fixed 11–20°, dual 21–30° (the captions name only the movable range explicitly). It is sign-based: take each house by its own sign's type, not by the lagna's. `[0:35:37]`
- A KP horary can't be taken when the native isn't pursuing the matter. Predict only after accounting for past events and the native's health. `[0:24:27]`

## Judging a house or bhava

- A bhava with an occupant is always judged through that occupant's star lord. The other rules, R4 among them, come in only when the bhava is empty. `[0:27:40]`
- When several planets occupy a bhava, the strongest one comes forward for it (cf. the leading planet below `(?)`). Even that planet brings trouble if the running dasha doesn't support it. `[0:27:40]`
- A house's leading planet governs it. An afflicted (e.g., retrograde) leader keeps the house from performing until it turns direct, whatever the other occupants' strength (even pushkara navamsha), unless the progressions strongly support it and its star lord is good. (How the leader is chosen isn't stated `(?)`; the example had it at the start of the sign.) `[0:22:36]`
- To judge a house, take its lord and the lord's star lord. If that star lord is retrograde and the lord has no exception of its own, the house is spoiled. `[0:04:06]`
- Sharing a house protects nothing: a clean planet, even in its own sign, does not save a co-occupant. Only a star-lord link counts; a planet affects the planets placed in its nakshatras. `[0:04:06]`
- Without R4, when a dasha lord's star lord is spoiled, the dasha spoils the bhava the lord occupies (a lord in the 7th spoils the 7th); the damage doesn't move to the star lord's bhava. The captions add "not the 8th" `(?)`. `[0:26:39]`

## Star lord and dasha delivery

- In a dasha, a planet in its own nakshatra gives its results through its sign lord, which stands in for the star lord. `[0:34:49]`
- Two planets each in their own nakshatra and in each other's signs give each other's results: one's dasha runs like the other's. Grade each by the lord it follows; following the 12th lord gives only about half, following the lagna lord (placed in the 3rd) gives average results. `[0:34:49]`
- A chain is only as good as its middle link: planets in a retrograde planet's nakshatras are spoiled through it, and a clean planet in the same house, even its leader, does not rescue them. Only an exception does, such as the retrograde planet sitting in the 10th (the 7th doesn't qualify); a link to the gain bhavas is a separate case. `[0:23:37]`
- Without that exception, a good co-occupant (lagna lord or not) adds only about 10–20 marks to Light. It cannot lift the result to even 50%, and the dasha turns negative when activated. `[0:24:37]`
- A planet whose star lord is debilitated cannot deliver; Jupiter in the star of a debilitated Venus gives nothing. `[0:57:25]`
- A planet that is the star lord of many planets is the chart's key influencer. If it is spoiled, the chart underperforms even when the houses, dashas and Light look excellent, and good lords such as the lagna, 5th and 9th lords cannot protect it. `[0:57:25]`
- A planet in a gandanta pada fails to perform (stated tentatively, as the only visible cause). `[0:56:24]`

## Rule 4 (R4)

- R4 applies when the dasha lord is not in its own sign and exactly one planet occupies its nakshatras. That planet then delivers the dasha directly from its own house (a lone Ketu in the 9th gives 9th-house results); a lord in its own sign never triggers R4. `[0:05:07]`
- If a second planet also occupies the lord's nakshatras (e.g., Rahu joins), R4 does not apply. `[0:08:10]`
- The delivering planet is judged at level 1 only: is it retrograde, is it with Rahu or Ketu. Its star lord is not checked, since that is the dasha lord itself; even a retrograde dasha lord hardly matters here and affects only its own Light (captions: "20%, 80%" `(?)`). `[0:06:08]`
- Under R4 a clean delivering planet gives 100%; if it is retrograde with no exception, the score drops straight to 30. The captions then give 60 because the planet is in its own house `(?)`, a score that no longer applies once a second planet cancels R4. `[0:08:10]`
- An empty bhava is decided by R4: the single planet in its lord's nakshatras carries it, good if that planet is good, spoiled if it is spoiled. The captions add that a debilitated planet there "stops" it `(?)`. `[0:10:12]`
- R4 is a special-case tool for dasha analysis and bhava analysis; planet strength itself uses the default. The default and R4 values are shown separately, and they change over time. `[0:09:12]`

## Retrograde checks, exceptions and inversion

- The retrograde check has two layers. First, the planet itself must not be retrograde and must not be afflicted by Rahu or Ketu; second, its star lord must not be retrograde. `[0:31:46]`
- Never go to a third level: a planet in Saturn's star is judged by Saturn, not by Saturn's own star lord, even when that star lord is afflicted (e.g., a Moon sharing a nakshatra with Ketu). `[0:30:46]`
- Rahu or Ketu afflicts a planet only when they share a nakshatra; the same house but a different nakshatra is no affliction. `[0:49:18]`
- A retrograde planet in its own house whose star lord is also in its own house, in the 11th, gets the exception and scores 80%; its house is then not spoiled. `[0:02:03]`
- A planet in a retrograde planet's star takes the retro effect unless it has an exception of its own; the star lord's exception does not pass to it. `[0:04:06]`
- Dashas of planets in a retrograde planet's nakshatras don't work properly; the dasha of a clean co-occupant, or of planets in its stars, does. The results invert for a functional malefic in the retrograde planet's star, which delivers its yoga while its bhava suffers; a functional benefic there simply fails. `[0:25:38]`
- A retrograde dasha lord whose star lord (e.g., Rahu) is unsound spoils the house it sits in, even the 7th with its lord in its own sign there (marriage, then divorce, then remarriage). The dasha lord needs dasha favorability of its own; co-occupants can't supply it. `[0:24:37]`
- Nearness to the Sun does not spoil a planet: close to the Sun it is direct, so there is no retro effect `(?)`. `[0:49:18]`

## Dasha timing and progression

- A yoga acts only in the dasha of the planets that form it. Without that dasha nothing happens, and those dashas must come at the right time of life. `[0:16:24]`
- No trait, e.g., intelligence, can be fixed from a single combination. Combine it with time, since a trait works only when the dasha activates it. `[0:28:42]`
- Judge a real chart with the full planetary positions and the progressions for the remaining dashas, never from house placements alone. `[0:54:23]`

## Light

- Light never uses R4. It is always calculated for the planet itself, from its own star lord (its "parent"). `[0:06:08]`
- For a planet in its own nakshatra or in parivartana, Light takes the sign lord in place of the star lord and gives it the same level-1 test: not retrograde, not with Rahu or Ketu, not disturbed. `[0:32:48]`
- Such a planet skips step 1 and gets that plus automatically: a genuine plus with no Rahu/Ketu link, a "dummy" plus otherwise `(?)`. Step 2 then decides: a good sign lord makes it good, otherwise it is spoiled. `[0:32:48]`
- In Light the own-nakshatra case counts as an escape worth 80% `(?)`. The captions then ask "60 or 80? should be 100", and say the benefit drops away in Performance `(?)`. `[0:33:48]`

## Viparita raja yoga and dusthana links

- Viparita raja yoga delivers even when the planet involved is afflicted by Rahu or by Ketu; Ketu's detachment does not stop it. Results may come unevenly but they do come, like a flood once the gate opens. `[0:14:19]`
- It works whichever planet occupies the house, and even with none. The captions mention an extra advantage worth "one mark more" `(?)`. `[0:15:20]`
- It cuts both ways: gains or losses come in a flood, extreme either way. `[0:16:24]`
- The dusthana where the lords meet colors the result: the 6th gives victory over opponents and appreciation from others, the 8th extreme gains from death-related events such as an inheritance, the 12th unexpected gains, possibly abroad. Strength rises from 6 to 8 to 12. `[0:18:27]`
- The 8th lord in the star of the 12th lord, with that lord in the 12th, is excellent. `[0:05:07]`
- A natural malefic whose star lord sits in a dusthana is normally good. `[0:57:25]`

## Maraka and badhaka

- A planet ruling both the 2nd and the 7th is a strong (prabala) maraka. Its link to the 6th, or to a trine lord placed in the 6th, is not a serious flaw. `[0:52:20]`
- A planet in the badhaka house that links to the 6th removes the badhaka flaw, so the badhaka lord can give results; such dusthana links are acceptable. `[0:53:22]`
- A maraka lord linked to the 6th does not by itself ruin health when the 6th lord is strong (captions: "3rd and 8th lord" `(?)`). `[0:49:18]`

## Houses and significations

- Money lost through a particular person is judged from that person's house first (the 7th for a spouse), not from the 6th. If that house is spoiled, whatever is given to that person is lost. `[0:01:01]`
- Money given to siblings or other relatives is judged from the 2nd lord (family). If the 2nd lord is spoiled, money given within the family is lost more or less regardless of the period; a good period might bring some back, but that can't be relied on. `[0:02:03]`
- A spoiled 7th can show as no marriage `(?)`, divorce, or two marriages. `[0:02:03]`
- The 2nd lord in the 12th brings family expenditure: spending on relatives' health, or money given to family and lost. `[0:03:04]`
- The lagna lord linked to the 10th and in exchange with the 11th lord shows earning through one's own effort. `[0:12:17]`
- A benefic aspect (Jupiter, Venus) on the lagna marks a better person. Jupiter's aspect on the lagna lord or the lagna's nakshatra lord `(?)` works the same way, as do benefic aspects on the Moon; a retrograde benefic's aspect still helps, graded by its dignity. `[0:19:31]`
- Sun and Moon together mean a new-moon (amavasya) birth; such a waning Moon should not be joined by Venus `(?)`. `[0:54:23]`
- Yoga practice (and a spiritual bent) comes from Jupiter, Ketu and Saturn, or from links to the moksha houses 4, 8 and 12; either is enough. Mars is not needed, and the same combinations hold however it is practised. `[0:35:51]`
